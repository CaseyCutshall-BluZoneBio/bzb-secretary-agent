# Sarah — how to use her

Sarah is your AI scheduling assistant. She finds a time with people outside BZB and books it on your calendar. She writes from her own address and never from yours.

## Set up (once, two minutes)

1. Open **https://bzb-ai-1.tail9f1964.ts.net:8443** and sign in with your Blu Zone Bio Microsoft account. (If it says sign-in didn't work, ask Casey to add you to "Sarah users".)
2. Click **Connect your calendar** and accept. This lets Sarah see when you're free, place private holds, and book meetings you confirm. She can't read your email.
3. Check **Settings**: your working hours and timezone come from Outlook; adjust meeting length, gaps, and how far ahead she looks. Save.

That's it. Use **Send me a test** on the home page if you want to see an email from her.

## Start

Reply to (or write) an email with the client on it, **CC `sarah.johnson@bluzonebio.com`**, and say something like:

> Sarah will find us a time.

You can add details and she'll use them:

- a length: "45 min", "an hour"
- a place: "on Teams", "in person at our office", "by phone"
- a window: "next week", "Thursday afternoon", "after the 20th"

Otherwise she uses your defaults from Settings (30 minutes on Teams unless you changed it).

Sarah replies to everyone, moves you to BCC, and offers three times that fit your calendar. While the client decides, she puts private "Hold" blocks on your calendar; they disappear on their own.

## Confirm

When the client picks a time, Sarah emails you:

> **Confirm: Dana Whitfield — Tue Oct 6, 2:00 PM EDT**

Reply:

- **YES** to book it. The invite goes to the client from your calendar, with a Teams link.
- **NO** and she'll offer them other times.
- or a different day or time ("how about Thursday?") and she'll offer that instead.

If it starts or ends right next to another meeting, she says so in that email.

## Take a thread back

Reply in the client thread with "I'll take it from here", and Sarah stops.

## When she hands it back to you

She stops and emails you **"Needs you: …"** when:

- the client asks something only you can answer
- the client wants a time in another timezone
- the client sends their own booking link
- there's no agreement after several rounds
- something unexpected happens

If appropriate, she tells the client you'll follow up. From then on the thread is yours.

## Away? Pause her

On the portal's home page, click **Pause Sarah**. While paused she ignores new requests from you and emails you that she did; threads already in progress continue. **Resume** when you're back.

## If she loses access to your calendar

You'll get one email, **"Action needed: reconnect your calendar to Sarah"**. Click the link and reconnect (two clicks). Until you do, she won't start anything new for you, and any thread that needed your calendar comes back to you as "Needs you".

## Good to know

- Sarah only works on emails **from you** with her on **To or CC**. She won't act on forwards or BCCs.
- She doesn't reschedule or cancel yet. If a client asks, it comes to you.
- After a meeting is booked, you can reply on the same email thread with "Sarah, find us a follow-up time" to start a new one.
- If a client needs a second round, options continue numbering (4, 5, 6…), so there's never confusion about which "option 2" they meant.
- Her emails are signed "Scheduling Assistant to *your name* (AI)", so clients know she's an assistant.
- **My threads** in the portal lists what she's working on for you.
